Airwave 1.3 includes everything from 1.2, which was not released on its own.

New in 1.3
- A lock screen switch (Settings › Playing › Show on the lock screen), off by default, so nothing shows on the lock screen unless you want it to.
- No notification question when the app first opens. Airwave asks only when you switch on something that needs notifications.
- An Android Auto page with four steps and a button that opens Android Auto's settings.
- On the car screen, Podcasts starts with your newest unplayed episodes and your downloads.
- Episode filters on every podcast's page: newest or oldest first, and hide played. Each podcast remembers its choice.

New in 1.2
- Station pictures fixed.
- Podcasts › Browse: 43,589 English-language podcasts built in, by category and sub-category, with World, UK, US, Australia, Canada, Ireland and New Zealand charts.
- "More like this" and episode search on every podcast's page, and a sleep timer that stops at the end of the episode.
- More UK stations placed in a region: 1,005 of 1,936, up from 879.
