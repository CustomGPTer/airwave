First release of Airwave, a radio and podcast app for Android.

- About 1,900 UK stations by region and town, and around 50,000 more worldwide
- Filters by category, place, language and sound quality that combine and save as one-tap chips
- Add your own stations by link or playlist file, and your own podcasts by name, feed link or OPML list
- Pause and rewind live radio; podcasts with downloads, a queue and playback speed
- Sleep timer, radio alarm, Bluetooth, Android Auto, casting, a widget, an equaliser and a backup file
- No adverts in the app, no accounts, no tracking

**Good to know:** stations that stream as HLS, which includes some BBC streams, play but cannot be rewound. Android Auto shows Airwave only after Unknown sources is ticked in its developer settings. See https://customgpter.github.io/airwave/#first

Install steps and full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/airwave/releases.html
