Free in Airwave, with adverts elsewhere

Airwave has no paid version and no adverts of its own. The well-known radio apps on Google Play show adverts and sell upgrades.

What Airwave does not do

Airwave is an Android app only and is not on Google Play. It does not record radio, it has no audiobooks or live sports rights of its own, and stations may still play their own adverts, as they do on any radio. A few stations only allow listening in their own app or their own country.
