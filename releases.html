What was added, improved and fixed in each version of Airwave, newest first. The current version is 1.0.

Version 1.3

Lock screen choice, podcast browsing and Android Auto help

LatestReleased 21 November 2026. Includes everything from 1.2, which was not released on its own.

Added in 1.3

  • A lock screen switch (Settings › Playing › Show on the lock screen), off by default.
  • An Android Auto page with four steps and a button that opens Android Auto's settings.
  • On the car screen, Podcasts starts with your newest unplayed episodes and your downloads.
  • Episode filters on every podcast's page: newest or oldest first, and hide played. Each podcast remembers its choice.

Changed in 1.3

  • No notification question when the app first opens. Airwave asks only when you switch on something that needs notifications.

Added in 1.2

  • Podcasts › Browse: 43,589 English-language podcasts built in, by category and sub-category, with World, UK, US, Australia, Canada, Ireland and New Zealand charts.
  • “More like this” and episode search on every podcast's page, and a sleep timer that stops at the end of the episode.
  • More UK stations placed in a region: 1,005 of 1,936, up from 879.
  • Station pictures fixed.

Version 1.0

First release

Released 18 November 2026

Added

  • About 1,900 UK stations built in, with the most-played stations from the rest of the world, refreshed from the open Radio Browser directory once a day.
  • UK stations sorted into regions, then towns and counties, with Stations near me.
  • A worldwide list by country, and a search that covers the whole live directory of around 50,000 stations.
  • Filters by category, UK region and town, language and sound quality, which can be combined and saved as one-tap chips.
  • Your own stations: a stream link or a .m3u or .pls playlist link, several at once from a pasted list or a file, or a link shared to Airwave from another app. Each link is checked before it is saved.
  • Your own podcasts: search by name, paste a feed link, or import an OPML list from another podcast app.
  • Pause and rewind for live radio, keeping the last 10, 30 or 60 minutes, with a step back of 30 seconds and a jump back to live.
  • Podcasts with a UK chart by category, episode downloads, a queue, five playback speeds, skip back and forward, a remembered place in each episode, and a notice when a new episode is out.
  • Favourites in folders, drag to reorder, and a recently played row.
  • The song that is playing, where the station sends it, with a history list.
  • A sleep timer with fade-out, and a radio alarm with days, volume, a gentle start, snooze, and the phone's own alarm sound as a fallback.
  • Lock-screen and notification controls, Bluetooth and headset buttons, Android Auto, casting, and a home-screen widget.
  • An equaliser with ready-made settings, bass boost and volume levelling.
  • Data saver, a data-use counter, and reconnecting by itself when the signal drops.
  • Stations that fail to play twice are taken out of the lists. Stations and whole categories can be hidden, and all of it can be undone.
  • A backup file for favourites, folders, stations, podcasts, filters, alarm and settings, with an automatic copy in the phone's Documents folder.
  • Light, dark and true-black themes, a grid or a list, three tile sizes, and a player that takes its colour from the station's logo.
  • A Help page inside the app, and a notice when a newer version is ready.

Known limits

  • Stations that stream as HLS, which includes some BBC streams, play but cannot be paused and rewound.
  • Android Auto shows Airwave only after Unknown sources is ticked in Android Auto's developer settings.
  • Casting needs Google Play services on the phone.

Installing and updating

  • When you download, Chrome may warn that the file might be harmful. It shows this warning for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway.
  • Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.
  • Google Play Protect may say it does not recognise the app, or block it, because Airwave does not come from Google Play. Choose More details, then Install anyway.
  • Open Airwave. It asks to show notifications, which is how the player appears on the lock screen. Then tap any station.
  • If the sound stops when the screen turns off, open the phone's Settings › Apps › Airwave › Battery and choose Unrestricted.
  • The ? on the Home tab opens Help, which explains everything, including how to add your own stations and podcasts.
  • To update, download the latest version and install it over the one you have. Your favourites, stations, podcasts and settings are kept. Airwave tells you on the Home tab when a newer version is ready.
  • Every version is signed with the same key, so Android accepts an update only if it comes from the same developer.
